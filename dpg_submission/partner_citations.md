## NRERI / MAE Naming Finding

Recommended wording for the DPG draft:

> Lao PDR Ministry of Agriculture and Environment (MAE), including the Natural Resources and Environment Research Institute (NRERI) and its Environmental Laboratory.

Rationale:

- The United Nations in Lao PDR states that the recent merger of the Ministry of Agriculture and Forestry with the Ministry of Natural Resources and Environment created the Ministry of Agriculture and Environment (MAE), and describes MAE as leading Lao PDR's work in sustainable agriculture and environmental protection.
- A 2025 Green Climate Fund/JICA document gives a more detailed restructuring note: MAF and MONRE merged to form MAE on 13 June 2025, and former ministry names should be used for pre-restructuring activities while new names apply after the merger.
- WHO Lao PDR cites Vanhna Phanphongsa as Deputy Head of Division at the Natural Resources and Environment Research Institute (NRERI), in an air-pollution context.
- Older air-quality and environmental monitoring materials cite NRERI under MONRE, which is expected because those materials predate the 2025 merger.
